# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pagevo to be a project where anyone can participate — filing an issue, asking a
question, or sending a patch — without being harassed or belittled. Everyone
contributing to this project is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and be kind. Technical disagreement is welcome and useful; contempt is not.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume the person on the other end is competent, busy, and working from different
  context than you.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. "No" to a patch is not a judgement of
  the person who wrote it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual attention or imagery in any project space.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull
  requests.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and to any place where someone is representing Pagevo.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to one of the repository's maintainers — for example by
opening a direct message on GitHub, or, if you have no other private channel, via
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new) for this
repository, which reaches maintainers privately even though this isn't a security
report. Reports are handled privately, and we won't share your identity with the
person reported without your agreement.

Maintainers will do what's proportionate: a private word, editing or removing content, a
temporary cooling-off period, or a permanent ban from the project. Maintainers who don't
follow this document are held to it too.
